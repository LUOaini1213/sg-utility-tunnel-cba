# Review checklist for a CST feasibility study

A planner's checklist for reviewing a common services tunnel (CST) feasibility study. Written for this
desk study; it is not agency guidance.

## Need and option definition
- [ ] Is the do-minimum a credible direct-buried or utility-duct layout, costed on the same basis?
- [ ] Is infrastructure load stated (utilities, pipe sizes, plot connections per km, expected openings per km per year) and evidenced?
- [ ] Are the utilities committed to the tunnel confirmed by each operator, including district cooling and pneumatic waste?

## Costs and benefits
- [ ] Are capital costs priced in one stated price year, with escalation method and contingency shown?
- [ ] Are both options' lifecycle costs included: O&M of the tunnel (ventilation, monitoring, security), renewals, and road openings?
- [ ] Are disruption (social) costs separated from agency cash costs, so the financial and economic cases can be read apart?
- [ ] Is the discount rate stated, sourced and tested; is the appraisal period justified against the tunnel's design life?
- [ ] Is residual value at the end of the appraisal period treated consistently?
- [ ] Are the breakeven values reported (openings per km per year, social cost per opening) and compared with local evidence?
- [ ] Does the sensitivity analysis show which assumptions flip the decision, not only the base NPV?

## Staging and schedule
- [ ] Is tunnel phasing aligned with site formation, road construction and development release?
- [ ] Is road pavement over the tunnel sequenced after the tunnel civil works (no reopening of new roads)?
- [ ] Is the critical path identified, and is float on development-driven activities known?
- [ ] Is the cost of building a phase ahead of demand (stranded capital) weighed against later retrofit cost?
- [ ] Are statutory approvals and protection-zone requirements in the programme?

## Interdependencies and operations
- [ ] Is there an interface matrix for co-located systems, with segregation, leak, heat and electromagnetic interference risks resolved in the cross-section?
- [ ] Are common-mode hazards (fire, flooding at portals and shafts) and emergency access covered?
- [ ] Is spare capacity reserved and justified?
- [ ] Are plot branch connections provided at build time, so lot connections do not reopen roads?
- [ ] Are the operator, access rights, permit process and O&M cost-sharing agreed?
